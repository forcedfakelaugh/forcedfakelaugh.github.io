## Image and note review, 28 October 2026

All three local images were visually inspected. Notes describe visible features rather than inferred construction details or an on-site visit. Material labels describe appearance; the exact composition of the TAA facade frames and opaque balcony edges is not asserted. The Hive name and photographer were rechecked against the original Unsplash page.

- **An angled dialogue**: the image shows staggered projecting balconies, solid parapets, planting and warm light. Its conceptual sketch now uses three offset volumes, replacing the unrelated inclined cutting plane. The inquiry is projection and overlap.
- **The rhythm of repetition**: the image shows large warm-toned frames, finer slats, recessed glazing and planting. The sketch uses repeated rectangular bays with slats and planted ledges, replacing the stepped tower. The regular grid is an imagined arrangement.
- **A space held open**: the image looks down the atrium of The Hive, with curved concrete edges, dark railings and hanging plants. The sketch abstracts the layered circulation as three open oval rings around a shared void; it is not the actual building plan.

Image notes, descriptions, drawing labels and accessibility text are authored in both Vietnamese and English. These are image-based interpretations and fictional design studies, not measured surveys or project specifications.

## Experience

Text-only project list with a clear current-project marker, previous/next project navigation, image/drawing switch, study notes dialog, image enlargement. Responsive single-column layout on small screens. Bitter and Source Sans 3 load from Google Fonts with local serif/sans-serif fallbacks. No application dependencies, backend or data collection.

Palette refinement: warm coral sidebar, sand background, dark brown text, inspired by TONIC.INC. Upright Bitter slab-serif headings and Source Sans 3 body type distinguish Nếp Architects from the other showcases. Navigation uses 13–16px text; main body copy uses 17px.
