# Mai Lê · artwork and reference

This is an original local portfolio concept for an imagined Hội An painter. Mai Lê, the paintings' titles, catalogue dates, sizes, studio and exhibition are fictional. The seven illustrations were generated for this concept with the built-in image generation tool, then converted to JPEG for local delivery. They are not photographs of physical oil paintings.

Visual and motion reference: [IMG_1202, uploaded by Eliza Yakymova](https://vimeo.com/1226352297), showing a Nina Elster artist-portfolio design. The reference informed the floating artwork entrance, spacious editorial gallery and exhibition invitation. No reference artwork, code or video is included in this project.

Fonts: DM Sans and Instrument Serif via Google Fonts, with system fallbacks. Shared portfolio return navigation uses ../portfolio-nav.css.

## Original artwork prompts

### assets/seascape.jpg

Use case: stylized-concept. Create a new original landscape oil painting inspired by the supplied seascape reference. The reference image is composition, subject and paint-texture inspiration only. A luminous late-afternoon ocean with deep blue and teal rolling waves, white impasto foam breaking across the foreground, a pale peach and pink sky with a soft gold sun, a shimmering golden reflection across the sea, and one graceful three-masted sailing ship in the upper right near the horizon. Make it a fresh related composition, with confident, thick palette-knife and brush marks, tactile canvas grain and luminous layered oil paint. Landscape format about 3:2. The actual painted seascape fills the entire output edge to edge as a flat frontal artwork reproduction. Exclude the easel, clamps, studio, canvas edges and photograph background seen in the source. No border, no frame, no signature, no text, no watermark. Finished fine-art painting, not a photo, not digital vector art.

The following prompts were sent individually to the built-in image generation tool. The seven final assets live under assets/ in this directory. The seascape uses the user-supplied ocean painting as a visual reference. The replacement self-portrait is an oil-painting transformation of the user-supplied studio photograph; its association with the fictional Mai Lê is part of the concept and is not a claim about the photographed person's name or authorship. Source uploads are not included in the site.

### assets/self-portrait.jpg

Use case: style-transfer. Create a genuinely hand-painted-looking self-portrait variation using the supplied existing portrait solely as a pose, wardrobe and studio composition reference. Subject: a slender young adult Asian woman, clearly in her twenties, with dark hair loosely tied back, delicate natural facial proportions, looking gently down at a paint palette in her hands; wearing a simple charcoal-brown sleeveless painter's apron. Keep the relaxed three-quarter standing pose, paintbrush, cream studio wall and soft pink canvas behind her, pale blue painting at the left. Retain the person's overall impression but prioritize a visibly expressive painting over exact photographic likeness. A lyrical post-impressionist oil colour study, loose flat shapes, broken edges and a soft hand-drawn quality. Painterly anatomy with slightly imperfect proportions, face suggested through broad blocks of warm cream, salmon and rose with blue-grey shadows. Leave a little warm underpainting showing between strokes. Muted lilac, butter yellow, dusty pink and ochre studio colours. Deliberately unfinished passages in hands and apron, still a finished gallery-worthy painting. No smooth gradients. Crucial: this must NOT be a photo with a paint texture overlaid. Reconstruct the entire face, neck, shoulders, arms and hands out of broad obvious paint strokes and simplified colour planes; no photorealistic face or skin, no smooth beauty retouching, no pores or individual eyelashes, no lens effects, no photographic lighting, no cinematic grading. The face must be as painterly as the surrounding room. Portrait aspect ratio 2:3, full bleed flat reproduction of a single painted canvas, no border, no frame, no text, no signature, no watermark.

Selected from three parallel variations with a stronger painted treatment. The current portrait uses the colour-study variation. All three images and prompts are retained in [PORTRAIT-OPTIONS.md](PORTRAIT-OPTIONS.md).
