## Implementation

Original layout, styling and interactions written for this concept. No code from Claude's showcase is used. Self-contained route: `/showcases/lumierreart-2/`. The small top bar and document title identify the requested name; the gallery retains the client's Lumiere Arts brand.

Google Fonts: Manrope and Be Vietnam Pro, with system fallbacks.

## Design references

The revised direction was informed by these live gallery websites, visited on 2026-10-27:

- https://francisgallery.com/ · restrained navigation, generous space around artwork and direct exhibition captions.
- https://mennour.com/ · exhibition images at different scales and a compact editorial hierarchy.
- https://www.bisougallery.com/ · a single distinctive background colour, minimal navigation and a clear gallery identity.

These informed the art direction only. No images, logos or source code from these reference sites are included. This concept uses a burgundy masthead, pale grey paper, an irregular artwork wall, and a switchable gallery/index catalogue.

The wall preview is illustrative, not a calibrated representation of dimensions. Saved selections are stored only in this browser. Inquiry actions compose a draft in the visitor's email client; they do not send mail automatically. Collection links and social links lead to the real brand's website and accounts.
