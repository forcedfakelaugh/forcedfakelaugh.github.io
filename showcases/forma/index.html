 ABOUT THIS CONCEPT

A way of looking.

FORMA imagines an architectural practice whose website behaves like an open sketchbook. A small index, a generous viewing surface, and enough context to look a little closer.

Browse the studies. Move between the photograph and a diagram. Notice how mass, rhythm, and light change what a space can become.
