01 — The idea

A song ends.
Something
stays on.

Dư ảnh is the Vietnamese word for an afterimage: the shape your eye still sees after you look away.

So the album has no fixed cover. It is one prism of light that changes with every side and moves with the sound.

Eight weeks in Hanoi and Saigon
  1. Week 1
