RECONSTRUCTED.

An environment you can manipulate. A system you can feel.

Tidal turns an ocean simulation into an interactive instrument. Tune the wind. Shift the atmosphere. Drop into the scene. This is a creative computing experiment at the intersection of physical systems and interface design.

002 / SYSTEM ANATOMY

BELOW THE SURFACE

THREE LAYERS. ONE WORLD.
A / DISPLACEMENT

Construct the sea.

Overlapping wave spectra shape the surface. Large swells establish the rhythm. Fine ripples break the reflection into thousands of highlights.

FFT WAVE SIMULATION
B / ATMOSPHERE

Scatter the light.

Atmospheric scattering, sky reflections, and bloom create a changing horizon. Three art-directed presets reinterpret the same environment.

LIGHT / SKY / REFLECTION
C / INTERACTION

Change the conditions.

Adjust wind speed, switch atmosphere, and look around in immersive mode. The scene responds directly through the WebGPU renderer.
