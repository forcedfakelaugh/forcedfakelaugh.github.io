03 / Art objects

Shaped by hand from Giang paper.

Paper pulp from Giang bamboo, a material associated with the H’mông community in Hòa Bình, blended with fine clay, lacquer-grounded and painted by hand. No two are alike.

04 / The artist

Nguyễn Văn Trinh

Imagined landscapes where the boundaries between reality, memory and imagination quietly dissolve.

Trinh layers watercolour on silk, letting its translucency meet the expressive energy of Giang paper. The works hold an enduring admiration for nature, and resist fixing emotion inside a single definition.

  1. SilkFrom silkworm cocoon to the painting ground.
  2. WashWatercolour built up in thin, luminous layers.
  3. GoldGold leaf laid by hand on selected works.
  4. SignatureThe last step that completes the work.
