# Lumiere Arts (redesign proposal)

A website redesign demo for Lumiere Arts (https://lumierearts.com/vi), built 27 October 2026 to show the client how their current Shopify site could be presented. Unlisted on the portfolio homepage.

## Content and photography

All artwork images, product photos, titles, sizes, years, prices and descriptions come from the Lumiere Arts public catalogue (`/products.json`) and homepage, and remain © Lumiere Arts / Nguyễn Văn Trinh. Images were downloaded from the Shopify CDN, resized to at most 1600 px and re-encoded as WebP in `assets/`. Copy is condensed from their product descriptions; series counts come from their collection listings.

Not from the source: the layout, interactions, true-scale wall preview (sofa drawing and room proportions) and favicon were authored for this proposal.

## Scope

Original prices are shown as "price on request", matching the live site's "Available upon request". Scarf and object prices are the listed USD prices. The inquiry form is simulated and sends nothing.
