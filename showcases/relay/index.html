    Field Notes

    Why we call it RELAY.

    The handoff

    Nobody runs well in isolation for long. You borrow pace from the person ahead of you, then hold it for whoever comes next.

    The amber seam

    No oversized chest logos. Just one 3mm signal-amber line tuned to sodium streetlights and motorbike high beams.

    The late window

    Designed for 28°C humidity and sudden pavement showers—ultralight black fabrics that breathe fast and dry on the run.
