# Alluvia gifting concept

Independent design proposal for outreach, created 1 November 2026. This is not an official Alluvia website, commissioned work, or an approved campaign. The page does not accept orders or send customer data. Gift quantity and note controls are a local interaction preview. Product links open the official website; preview quantities and notes are not transferred.

## Product details

The official gifting catalogue listed these prices on 1 November 2026: Vietnam Signature Giftbox 16pcs 235,000 VND; Mekong Essence 9 pcs 175,000 VND; Landmark 10pcs 150,000 VND; Napolitan 10pcs 140,000 VND. The interface explicitly calls these reference prices and sends visitors to the official product page for current price, availability, and actual ordering. No discounts, stock status, reviews, sustainability certifications, or commercial outcomes are asserted.

Editorial gift positioning, headlines, and suggested gift notes are original proposed copy. The cacao-process description is based on the official homepage's harvest, fermentation, drying, roasting, and grinding sequence. No shipping, custom packaging, note-printing, or gift personalization service is promised by this demo.

## Interaction and accessibility

Budget filters operate on four verified product prices. Selecting a product opens a native modal dialog with quantity controls (1 through 99), a 180-character local note preview, calculated reference total, and an official product link. Native dialog keyboard focus handling, Escape dismissal, explicit close, focus restoration, reduced-motion support, visible keyboard focus, semantic headings, labelled controls, and live result announcements are provided. No third-party scripts, trackers, remote fonts, form endpoints, or network-based interactions are included.

## Redesign references, inspected 1 November 2026

- https://tonyschocolonely.com/ and https://uk.tonyschocolonely.com/: inspected the live page contents, published HTML section structure, and published stylesheet. The UK page leads with a prominent gifting campaign, moves directly into product browsing, and separates the cocoa story from the shop. Its strong red/yellow color fields and forceful display type informed this proposal's packaging-scale hierarchy. This Alluvia design uses its own Vietnamese wording, a rotated Alluvia gift photograph, original shelf interaction, and Alluvia's red/gold palette. No Tony's images, fonts, text, layout source, sustainability claims, or offers are reused.
- https://lechocolatdesfrancais.fr/ and https://lechocolatdesfrancais.fr/collections/coffrets-chocolat: inspected the actual homepage and gift-box collection content. Their attention to illustrated packaging, occasions, and budget informed presenting each Alluvia box as a distinct gift and keeping budget selection close to the products. No images, illustrations, reviews, personalization service claims, shipping promises, or promotional language are reused.

The revised presentation is a bold Vietnamese gift poster: red field, oversized yellow sans typography, a central photographic composition, numbered single-product shelf, and immersive farm photograph. The earlier cream/serif card-grid presentation is replaced. Shelf controls use keyboard-operable tabs; budget filters also update the selected visible product. These are original layout and interaction decisions for this independent Alluvia proposal.

## Impeccable refinement, 1 November 2026

Refined using the [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable) polish and craft guidance.

The polish preserves the red and yellow gift-poster direction while separating the package caption from the closing headline. All four gift tabs remain visible in the mobile shelf. Product images decode before the shelf updates, failed loads can be retried, and rapid budget changes keep the visible product consistent. Each gift retains its quantity and note while the page stays open. The preview close action remains visible during dialog scrolling.
