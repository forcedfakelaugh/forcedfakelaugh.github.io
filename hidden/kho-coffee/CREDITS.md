# K’Ho Coffee proposal: sources and asset credits

Independent, uncommissioned website proposal for private outreach. This page is not the official K’Ho Coffee website. K’Ho Coffee and its respective photographers retain rights to all brand photography. Assets were downloaded from the brand’s public website and resized/cropped for this proposal only. No stock or generated imagery is used.

Checked on 1 November 2026.

## Official factual sources

- [K’Ho Coffee home](https://www.khocoffee.com/home): cooperative of families growing Arabica on Langbiang Mountain, Dalat.
- [Our Coffee](https://www.khocoffee.com/new-page): farm-based roasting, heirloom varieties, harvesting and processing, washed/natural/honey methods, farm shop.
- [Order Coffee](https://www.khocoffee.com/menu): 250 g at 200,000 VND, 500 g at 400,000 VND, 1 kg at 800,000 VND; prices exclude VAT and shipping. Light/City roast for pour-over with cinnamon/citrus/almond; Medium/Vienna for espresso/drip with caramel/almond/chocolate; Dark/French for French press with dark chocolate/smoky/malt. Availability is not asserted. The interactive selection never places an order or sends data.

## Design references inspected online

- [Onyx Coffee Lab](https://onyxcoffeelab.com/): producer spotlight connects a specific farm/family story with an offering; the site exposes roast-finding and brewing tools alongside commerce. Applied the content principle of origin first and helpful choice second, with K’Ho’s own photography and facts.
- [Onyx Find My Roast](https://onyxcoffeelab.com/pages/find-my-roast): reference for giving a coffee chooser its own clear task. K’Ho’s simple brew-method mapping comes from K’Ho’s published menu, not Onyx’s recommendation logic.
- [Origin Coffee](https://www.origincoffee.co.uk/): reviewed current site and source, including shop navigation by brew method and editorial learning paths around producers, varietals and processing.
- [Origin Direct Trade](https://www.origincoffee.co.uk/pages/direct-trade): the image-supported chapter structure and links between price, relationships and traceability informed a compact photographic journal with factual captions. No Origin claims, imagery or copy were reused.

The final composition is an original full-bleed farm landscape, dark pine contact sheet, restrained sans-serif typography and monospaced annotations. The chooser is a paper order slip over a real farm-shop image. It deliberately avoids the cream split hero, large italic serif headings and repeated product-card grid used elsewhere in the portfolio.

System Arial/Helvetica and Courier New font stacks require no downloaded font assets. No personal information is collected. Official-site links are clearly identified.

## Impeccable refinement, 1 November 2026

Refined using the [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable) polish and craft guidance.

The polish preserves the green farm-journal direction and gives the coffee selection panel more room. Brew-method choices stack on mobile, quantity controls have larger touch targets, and calculated totals use tabular numerals. The preview opens at the top, keeps its close action visible during scrolling, and restores focus when closed.
