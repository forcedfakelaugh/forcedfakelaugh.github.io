## Copy and facts

The editorial copy is newly written in Vietnamese, paraphrasing the official source pages above. The 2004 kiln opening in Hiên Vân, Bắc Ninh, Lý/Trần/Lê/Mạc references, temple-carving inspiration and rice-hull/wood-ash glazes come from the official About page. Product names and descriptions come from their corresponding linked articles. Glaze labels are descriptive labels consistent with the official Bình bổ ô article and the photographed pieces. Images are presented as archive imagery, not a claim of stock availability.

The showroom address, 8 Chân Cầm in Hà Nội, comes from https://hienvanceramics.com/contact/. Availability, dimensions, sales claims and prices are deliberately not asserted. Contact links lead to the official website. The wordmark is a typographic treatment for this proposal, not a downloaded brand logo. Catalogue numbers H.01 to H.08 are navigation labels created for this proposal, not historical accession numbers or official product codes.

## Online design references for the revised archive

The first cream editorial layout was replaced in response to the user's feedback. These live references were researched on 1 November 2026. They informed composition and information architecture only; no images, typography files, copy, logos, or code were borrowed from these sites.

- https://1616arita.jp/en/ : the parent's browser inspection found a large object photograph and compact navigation. The site also separates object journals from product navigation. The revised opening gives one ceramic photograph a quiet central position, with a small contextual label instead of a marketing headline and split hero.
- https://www.vam.ac.uk/collections/ceramics : museum collection highlights pair objects with concise material and context labels, then lead to deeper object records. This informed the small H.01-style catalogue labels and full object-detail dialogue.
- https://collections.vam.ac.uk/search/?q=ceramics : collection browsing exposes filters and object-specific records. This informed the functional object-type filters and the ability to switch between an irregular exhibition view and a compact catalogue list.
- https://collections.centreofceramicart.org.uk/ : this public studio-ceramics archive distinguishes the collection, context, and access information. This informed separating the photographed objects, the workshop notebook, and the visiting address.

The deep plum-brown gallery, varied image scales, irregular spacing, restrained serif object titles and taupe workshop notebook are original art-direction choices for Hiên Vân. The full-image compositions preserve the original subjects and watermarks. The page describes an independent website concept, not a claim that Hiên Vân operates a museum.

## Implementation

Self-contained HTML, CSS and JavaScript. System Georgia, Arial and Courier New fonts; no external font requests. Responsive local WebP sources, native dialog accessibility and keyboard Escape, reduced-motion support, category filters, exhibition/catalogue view switching and an editable copy-only inquiry preview. `noindex,nofollow`, parent Open Graph metadata, favicon and an independent proposal label are present.

## Impeccable refinement, 1 November 2026

Refined using the [Impeccable](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable) polish and craft guidance.

The polish preserves the quiet archival presentation with clearer type, roomier mobile photographs, and a compact gallery toolbar. Filtered and list views adapt to narrow screens. Product details use responsive image sources, saved-item feedback appears inside the modal, and dialog focus returns to the relevant opener. The notebook and visit section have more readable mobile spacing.
